Anime conventions around the world

Move the Year slider below the map to explore when listed conventions were founded. Choose Founded by this year to see their cumulative spread, or Founded in this year to see only that year's new listings.

Each dot represents a listed convention at its listed city centre. Conventions in the same city overlap. Hover over a dot for its name, founding year and other listings at that location.

Data coverage: 102 convention/location entries across 21 countries, with founding years from 1975 to 2024. This is a selective directory; it does not count all conventions worldwide or annual editions, and the cumulative view does not establish that a convention remained active or was historically held in the mapped city. The separate defunct/on-hiatus table is excluded.
